# What the 2026 crack / thin-structure literature actually offers

*2026-09-19. From a 9-agent sweep; every reference was resolved live against the arXiv API or
Crossref and quoted from the retrieved record. **No number below is compared to ours** —
different datasets, labels and operating points. What transfers is methods and protocols.*

## The direct answer to "is our model the best?"

**No, and there is a specific publicly-available model that is the one to beat.**

**OmniCrack30k** (Benz & Rodehorst, CVPRW 2024, `10.1109/CVPRW63382.2024.00392`) trains
nnU-Net on **30,017 samples / 9.03 billion pixels / 20 subsets** spanning asphalt, ceramic,
concrete, masonry **and steel**, and reports *"nnU-Net achieves a mean clIoU4px of 64%
outperforming all other approaches by at least 10% points"*. Weights are publicly released.
Its own conclusion is that *"architectural modifications are of subordinate relevance"* and
*"The standout model is nnU-Net, relying on the basic U-Net architecture."*

That is the arm this corpus has not run. It is a fair candidate precisely because it was
trained across materials including steel, and because clIoU4px is the metric this project has
now adopted. **Running it on the 15 tiles is the single highest-value remaining experiment
after SERD**, and it is the honest way to answer the accuracy question — the same model, our
data, our protocol.

## The one to run first, because it targets our exact failure

**SERD** — *Rapid-Deployment Crack Measurement Based on SAM3 Semantic-Edge Response Decoding*,
`arXiv:2607.12292` (v1 14 Jul 2026, v4 6 Sep 2026). Verbatim:

> *"We identify an output-interface mismatch in SAM3: its prompt-conditioned semantic response
> preserves crack evidence that is often suppressed or spatially distorted in the final
> candidate masks. Across six public crack datasets, the internal response achieves **82.66%**
> average crack-pixel recall, compared with **74.66%** for the retained SAM3 proposals, with an
> average mismatch ratio of 8.73%."*

**Why this is mechanically certain to matter here, not just plausible.** In the SAM 3 code this
project runs (`sam3_image_processor.py:193-201`), the presence scalar multiplies `pred_logits`
**only**; `outputs["pred_masks"]` is never touched by the presence head, and the mask tensor is
filtered solely by the `keep` line. So the 105× presence span measured here, and both tiles
that returned nothing, are artefacts of `keep` alone. Reading `pred_masks` before `keep` is
**presence-invariant by construction** and removes the "returns nothing" failure mode entirely,
because a continuous field has no empty case.

Recipe (training-free, no learned parameters, ~6 ms/image):
`Re = Norm( Rq ⊙ (1 + E) )`, where `Rq` is the dense concept-conditioned field (sigmoid,
bilinear-resized, per-image min-max normalised) and `E` is the normalised 3×3 Sobel gradient
magnitude.

**CoRe-SAM3**, `arXiv:2609.05816` (5 Sep 2026 — the newest crack-segmentation paper on arXiv at
time of sweep) reaches the same place: *"the semantic representation already carries most task
information for crack prediction, whereas the utility of the visual representation depends on
the current semantic state. Directly combining the two representations does not yield
consistent gains."* Its trained residual head (18,914 params) is out of scope here, but its
zero-shot arm is exactly the SERD interface.

## Supervised state of the art (needs training; take the protocol, not the weights)

| work | venue / id | note |
|---|---|---|
| RIFT | `arXiv:2605.31048` | *"cracks have limited category-level semantics but strong morphological regularities"* — argues explicitly against the backbone arms race. RIFT-T: 0.47 M params, 1.39 GFLOPs, 355 FPS |
| MixerCSeg | `arXiv:2603.01361`, CVPR 2026 | CNN + Transformer + Mamba, 2.54 M params |
| SCRWKV | `arXiv:2605.14926`, ICML 2026 | 1.22 M params, linear complexity |
| SCSegamba | `arXiv:2503.01113`, CVPR 2025 | 2.8 M params; source of the TUT benchmark |
| CrackGeoFM | `arXiv:2606.05641` | frozen backbone + crack adaptation over 20 datasets; **five-image few-shot** is the interesting number for a corpus this size |

**TUT** is the most relevant public benchmark to this material: 1408 images, eight scenarios
**including metal**, blades and pipes.

## Topology-aware metrics and losses — the family this project has now adopted

- **clDice**, `arXiv:2003.07311`, CVPR 2021, `10.1109/CVPR46437.2021.01629` — *"we theoretically
  prove that clDice guarantees topology preservation up to homotopy equivalence"*. Already the
  primary metric here.
- **clIoU_τ**, OmniCrack30k — width-insensitive tolerant IoU. Implemented here in
  `tolerance_sweep.py`.
- **SEMIR**, `arXiv:2606.24935`, ECCV 2026 — written for this exact regime: *"Thin-structure
  segmentation — power lines, cracks, lane markings at 1-3 pixel width — requires preserving
  connectivity that standard representations preclude"*; reduces mask fragmentation ≥4.6× vs
  SLIC.
- **WPRF**, `arXiv:2607.07123` — names *"topological gradient starvation"*: *"pixel-wise losses
  distribute gradients uniformly, yet connectivity hinges on a sparse set of bottleneck
  pixels"*; plug-and-play, clDice +7.2 pp on fragile datasets.
- **Crack Topology Score**, `arXiv:2601.10762` — *"pixel-wise metrics such as IoU or F1-score
  fail to capture structural validity"*. Independent confirmation of this project's measured
  IoU ceiling of 0.17.

## Label-free

**Crack-Segmenter** (`arXiv:2510.10378`; *Automation in Construction* 181:106591) claims
pixel-level crack segmentation *"entirely without manual annotations"* and to outperform 13
supervised methods across ten datasets. Treat the comparative claim with suspicion; the module
design is usable.

## What this changes for this project

1. **Run SERD** — in progress. Mechanically targeted at the failure mode measured here.
2. **Run OmniCrack30k's released nnU-Net on the 15 tiles.** This is how to answer "is it the
   best" honestly: same model, our data, our protocol, clIoU4px.
3. **Keep clDice and clIoU_τ as primary.** The literature and this corpus's own 0.17 IoU
   ceiling agree that pixel IoU cannot rank methods here.
4. **Do not chase architectures.** OmniCrack30k and RIFT independently conclude that
   architecture is secondary; data scale and morphology-aware objectives are what move results.
