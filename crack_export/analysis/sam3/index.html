This is the offline evaluation detector, not the detector the app ships. 62 frames, ridge filter on raw images, no hand corrections applied. The app’s two-pass pipeline is a different detector and is not benchmarked against the published arms — it cannot be, from committed artefacts, because corrections are baked into its output and 98% of the evaluation frames are in its training set (POSITION_VS_2026.md §11b). Do not read this page as “how well the app does”.
